Build Asteroids

A construction tutorial for Asteroids and Asteroids II — the same two games in this collection, rebuilt here one small piece at a time. Each lesson adds one mechanic, shows the exact code for it, and gives you a live, playable "game so far" to try before moving on.

Core: Building Asteroids from Scratch

Twelve lessons, plain JavaScript and a <canvas> element, zero frameworks — ending with the same game shipped as asteroids.html.

1

A resizing full-screen canvas and a requestAnimationFrame loop that ticks with real elapsed time

2

3
Turning and Thrust
Keyboard input, heading, and accumulating a velocity vector with drag
›
4
The Camera Trick
Keeping the ship pinned to screen-center by moving the world instead of the ship
›
5
Asteroids and Wraparound
Spawning drifting rocks in an unbounded world and wrapping them around the camera
›
6
Shooting
Bullets, a fire-rate cooldown, and giving projectiles a lifespan
›
7
Collisions
Circle-vs-circle distance checks between bullets, rocks, and the ship
›
8
Splitting Rocks and Scoring
Big rocks break into smaller ones, with points that scale as they shrink
›
9
Lives, Game Over, Restart
Respawn invulnerability, a game-over overlay, and resetting state cleanly
›
10
Persisted High Scores
Saving and loading a best score with localStorage
›
11
The Mobile Control Bar
A three-zone touch bar — hold-to-turn and tap-fire/hold-thrust, with a keyboard fallback
›
12
Mobile Hardening
The real bugs shipping to phones surfaces: stuck buttons, the long-press magnifier, accidental pinch-zoom
›
